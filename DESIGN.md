# potong.ai design direction

## Identity

potong.ai should feel like a practical editing desk for Malaysian creators, not a generic AI SaaS landing page. The product is about finding the useful cut quickly, so the interface should be direct, editorial and workmanlike.

## Personality

- Clear, calm and fast
- Slightly editorial, never futuristic
- Human Bahasa Melayu Malaysia
- Useful before impressive


## Design read

- ENERGY 2/5: calm workspace with one strong action color
- RHYTHM 3/5: large working area, compact control rail, then dense edit-decision rows
- MOTION 1/5: only progress and interaction feedback

The focal point is the upload/work area. The red accent behaves like an edit marker: it appears on the primary action, progress and metadata, not as decoration. Small uppercase monospace labels are reserved for production metadata such as NEW CUT and job IDs, so the treatment reads like an edit decision list rather than a generic tech heading.

## Visual language

- Warm paper background, near-black ink and one cut-red accent
- Red is reserved for actions, progress and edit markers
- Sharp or lightly rounded geometry, never pill-heavy
- Minimal shadows; hierarchy comes from spacing, rules and type
- Timecodes use a compact monospace treatment
- The wordmark is typographic only: `potong / ai`
- No gradients, glow, glassmorphism, dot grids, decorative blobs or fake product art

## Composition

The first screen is the product, not a marketing page. Show the upload workspace, real settings, worker status and real processing states. Avoid feature-card grids, bento layouts, fake statistics, testimonials and template "how it works" sections.

Results should read like an edit decision list: index, timecode, title, reason, preview and download. Use rows instead of a gallery of repeated cards.

## Motion

Motion only communicates state: progress, hover, focus and button press. No entrance animation, floating decoration or looping motion.

## Copy

Use Bahasa Melayu Malaysia with familiar production terms where useful. Keep sentences short. Avoid buzzwords such as "revolutionary", "seamless", "next-generation" and "AI-powered". Do not invent performance claims or a "viral" score. Call the internal ranking a clip score.

## Responsive

Mobile is a distinct layout, not a squeezed desktop. Controls stack, results reflow, type scales down and all interactive targets remain at least 44px tall.
